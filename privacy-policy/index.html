Privacy policy

This policy covers designs.niveshsahayak.in, a website run by Nivesh Sahayak that shows website designs and takes requests for them.

What we collect

Nothing you type on this site is stored by us. The order form builds a message inside your own browser. When you press the button, WhatsApp opens with that message, and it reaches us only if you send it. From then on WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies to the chat.

Visitor counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to pages. It does not use cookies and does not follow you to other websites.

Things saved in your browser

Your light or dark choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's internet address when the fonts download.

Sample websites

The sample websites use made-up names, numbers and addresses. Their contact forms and buttons do not send anything.
