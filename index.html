Questions

Which platform do the designs use?

Every design is made for Blogger only, Google’s free website platform. It is not for WordPress, Wix or any other platform. You need a free Google account and a Blogger blog (we can set it up with you). There is no hosting bill, you can change text, prices and photos yourself from the Blogger dashboard, and your own domain name can be connected to it.

How much does a website cost?

A ready design made for your business costs ₹1,000. If you also want us to buy and connect your domain name and put the website online for you, tell us on WhatsApp and we will tell you the price for that.

Can I change the colours, text and photos?

Yes. Every design takes your business name, logo, colours, photos, products and timings. The samples only show how it can look.

My business is not in the list. Can you still make a website?

Yes. Pick the design you like best from any business type, or ask for a custom design. Tell us about your business on WhatsApp.

Do the sample businesses exist?

No. Every name, phone number, address and price in the samples is made up, so you can see the design with realistic content.

How do I pay?

We first agree on the design, the pages and the price on WhatsApp. You pay by UPI only after that.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every design is made for phones first and checked on small and large screens, in light and dark mode.

Do not see your type of business?

Tell us what you do. We will suggest a design or make a custom one.
