What a salon website needs

  1. Service price list

    Hair, skin, nails, waxing, threading, makeup, spa. Starting prices are fine where the final price depends on hair length.

  2. Bridal and party packages

    What each package includes, trial sessions and how far in advance to book.

  3. Appointment booking

    A form for service, date and time that sends to your WhatsApp. Confirm the slot yourself so there are no double bookings.

  4. Gallery of your work

    Hairstyles, bridal looks and nail art, with the client's permission.

  5. Hygiene and products

    Sterilised tools, disposable items and the kind of products you use.

  6. Timings and location

    Including ladies-only or unisex details, and home service if you offer it.

Tips before you go live

  • Ask clients before using their photos, especially bridal photos. A short consent line on your booking form helps.
  • Avoid promising permanent results for skin or hair treatments. Unsupported claims can be called misleading advertising.
  • Keep the price list as text so you can change it in a minute when rates change.
