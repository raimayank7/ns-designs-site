What a agro business website needs

  1. Products by crop or use

    Seeds, fertilisers, crop protection, equipment, feed. Group them by crop or season, not only by name.

  2. Licence details

    Seed, fertiliser and pesticide dealer licence numbers, which farmers and officials look for.

  3. Crop calendar and advice

    Short, practical notes for each season. They bring farmers back.

  4. Dealer and farmer enquiries

    A form or WhatsApp button for orders and dealership.

  5. Local language

    Hindi, Punjabi or the local language next to English.

  6. Location and delivery

    Shop and godown address, delivery to villages if you offer it.

Tips before you go live

  • Seed, fertiliser and pesticide dealers need licences under the Seeds Act and Seeds (Control) Order, the Fertiliser (Control) Order and the Insecticides Act. Show your licence numbers.
  • Do not promise exact yield increases. Describe what a product does and refer to the label and the agriculture department's advice.
  • For pesticides, advise reading the approved label and using safety gear. Never suggest doses beyond the label.

Questions

Can the website be in Punjabi or Hindi?

Yes. Many agro websites work best with a local language first and English second.

Can farmers order on WhatsApp?

Yes. The order button opens WhatsApp with the product name filled in.

Can I post seasonal advice?

Yes. Advice notes are Blogger posts you can write from your phone.

Which platform is the website made for?

Every design is made for Blogger only, Google’s free website platform. It is not for WordPress, Wix or any other platform. You need a free Google account and a Blogger blog (we can set it up with you). There is no hosting bill, you can change text, prices and photos yourself from the Blogger dashboard, and your own domain name can be connected to it.
