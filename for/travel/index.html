What a travel agent website needs

  1. Tour packages

    Destination, days and nights, what is included and excluded, price per person and the best season.

  2. Day-by-day itinerary

    Short and realistic: travel times, hotels category, meals.

  3. Other services

    Air and train tickets, taxis, hotel bookings, visa help, group tours, pilgrimages.

  4. Enquiry form

    Destination, dates, number of travellers, budget. Sent to your WhatsApp.

  5. Payment and cancellation policy

    Advance, balance and refund rules in writing.

  6. Trust details

    Office address, years in business, GSTIN, any tourism department approval or association membership you really hold.

Tips before you go live

  • Say "starting from" with the date the price is valid, because hotel and flight prices change.
  • Mention approvals only if you hold them (for example a Ministry of Tourism approval or IATA accreditation). Most travel businesses do not need them, but claiming them falsely damages trust.
  • Use your own trip photos or properly licensed ones. Copying photos from other travel websites is not allowed.

Questions

Can I show many packages?

Yes. Each package is a post with its own page, photos and itinerary.

Can customers pay online?

The design takes enquiries; you send a quote and payment details. Online payment can be added later.

Can I add group tour dates?

Yes, as a simple list of upcoming departures.

Which platform is the website made for?

Every design is made for Blogger only, Google’s free website platform. It is not for WordPress, Wix or any other platform. You need a free Google account and a Blogger blog (we can set it up with you). There is no hosting bill, you can change text, prices and photos yourself from the Blogger dashboard, and your own domain name can be connected to it.
