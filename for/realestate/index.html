What a property dealer website needs

  1. Listings

    Each property with type, locality, area, price or rent, floor, facing and a few photos.

  2. Buy, sell, rent filters

    Even a simple split into "For sale" and "For rent" helps visitors.

  3. Site visit booking

    A form with the property name already filled in.

  4. RERA registration

    Your agent registration number with your state's RERA authority, and the project registration number on every new-project listing.

  5. Owners: list your property

    A form for owners who want you to sell or rent their property.

  6. Office location and areas you cover

    Map link and the localities you know best.

Tips before you go live

  • Under the RERA Act, 2016 (section 9), real estate agents must register with the state authority and quote their registration number in the sales they facilitate; many states also require it in every advertisement, along with the project's RERA number. Check your state's rules.
  • Do not list new projects that are not registered with RERA where registration is required.
  • Take the owner's permission before publishing photos or the exact address of a property.

Questions

Can I add new listings myself?

Yes. Each listing is a Blogger post with photos and details, so adding one is like writing an email.

Can buyers filter by budget?

The basic design groups listings by sale and rent and by type. Budget filters can be added later.

Where do I show my RERA number?

In the header strip, the footer and on every new-project listing.

Which platform is the website made for?

Every design is made for Blogger only, Google’s free website platform. It is not for WordPress, Wix or any other platform. You need a free Google account and a Blogger blog (we can set it up with you). There is no hosting bill, you can change text, prices and photos yourself from the Blogger dashboard, and your own domain name can be connected to it.
