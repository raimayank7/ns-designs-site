What a wholesaler website needs

  1. Product categories

    What you trade in, grouped clearly, with pack sizes.

  2. Brands and agencies

    Brands you distribute, in plain text, only where you hold the dealership.

  3. Become a dealer

    An enquiry form for shopkeepers with their shop name, city and GSTIN.

  4. Minimum order, credit and delivery

    Delivery areas, days and vehicle, minimum order and payment terms in general words.

  5. Rate list on request

    Send the latest rate list on WhatsApp instead of publishing prices.

  6. Company details

    GSTIN, address, godown location and years in business.

Tips before you go live

  • Food traders need an FSSAI licence and should show its number. Check the licences your trade needs.
  • Do not publish your trade rates openly; competitors read them too. Offer "rate list on WhatsApp" for registered dealers.
  • Use brand names as text only where you are the appointed dealer or distributor.

Questions

Should I show prices?

Usually no. Wholesalers share rate lists with registered dealers on WhatsApp, and the site has a button for that.

Can new dealers apply on the website?

Yes. The dealer form collects shop name, city and GSTIN.

Can I list the brands I distribute?

Yes, as plain names where you hold the dealership.

Which platform is the website made for?

Every design is made for Blogger only, Google’s free website platform. It is not for WordPress, Wix or any other platform. You need a free Google account and a Blogger blog (we can set it up with you). There is no hosting bill, you can change text, prices and photos yourself from the Blogger dashboard, and your own domain name can be connected to it.
