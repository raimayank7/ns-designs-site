What a event planner website needs

  1. Services

    Wedding planning, decor, catering coordination, venue booking, entertainment, corporate events, birthdays.

  2. Portfolio gallery

    Your best events with a line on each: type, guests, city.

  3. Packages or starting prices

    Even a "starting from" helps families plan.

  4. How you work

    Steps from first meeting to the event day, so clients know what to expect.

  5. Enquiry form

    Event type, date, city, guests and budget range.

  6. Team and partners

    The people who will be there on the day.

Tips before you go live

  • Use photos from your own events, with the families' permission. Do not copy photos from other planners or decorators.
  • Put your advance, payment schedule and cancellation policy in writing on the website and in your agreement.
  • Show the cities you work in, so out-of-town enquiries know whether you travel.

Which platform is the website made for?

Every design is made for Blogger only, Google’s free website platform. It is not for WordPress, Wix or any other platform. You need a free Google account and a Blogger blog (we can set it up with you). There is no hosting bill, you can change text, prices and photos yourself from the Blogger dashboard, and your own domain name can be connected to it.
