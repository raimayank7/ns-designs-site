Three decades on the GT Road

Zaika started as a four-table dhaba for truck drivers in 1994. Today families stop on their way to the hills, and the drivers still get their tea first.

Everything is cooked in our own kitchen, in front of you, on a coal tandoor and big iron kadhais.
